Good questions

Questions, Answered

What Lightning does today, and what it doesn’t yet.

Can I try it today?

Yes, with sample data. Current Status has the Windows beta and Mohab’s sample files. Please don’t import a real bank statement yet.

Which computers does it run on?

Windows. The beta is a ZIP: unzip it and open Lightning. Other systems come later.

Is there a phone app?

Not yet. Add spending as you go on your computer, or import your bank’s CSV at the end of the month.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. Lightning never asks for bank logins. Download a CSV from any bank or wallet, match its columns once, and review every row before it’s saved.

What goes online?

Your data stays in an encrypted profile on your computer. The only thing Lightning looks up online is the latest price of listed shares, by ticker symbol.

Who is building Lightning?

Someone from finance in Egypt who has logged every pound since graduating. Excel took too long and budgeting apps missed the full picture, so Lightning does both. The full story is on Current Status.
