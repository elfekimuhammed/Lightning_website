A better system

You Don’t Suck at Budgeting

You Don’t Have a System

You might convince yourself that you do have a system by creating a budget, but two are not the same.

You on a Friday when you are budgeting to reach financial freedom and you when you are stressed out at work ordering a vanilla shake to feel better are not the same person.

You don’t save more by having better budgets, you save more by understanding your spending patterns and cutting out the unavoidable.

Study your patterns, and create better habits.

Or else your stressed out version will keep cursing you and your budget.
