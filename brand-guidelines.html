01 · Foundation

The Brand Idea

Meadow is a soft, welcoming environment with one vivid green to blue focal surface. Pale green signals growth, pale blue signals clarity, and deep Nile anchors actions.

Show the Pattern

Make money movement visible: what comes in, what goes out and what remains.

Make It Human

Talk about decisions and habits without blame. Give the reader a practical next step.

Keep It Honest

Label hypothetical returns clearly. Use examples to teach, never to promise an outcome.

06 · Call to action

Help Shape Lightning

One primary action and one optional alternative. Keep email and survey paths distinct.

Use a 22 px container radius, 14–18 px grid gap and 24–40 px internal padding. Keep at least 16 px between headings and dense content. Avoid unrelated metrics inside quote cards.

05 · Language

Voice and Tone

Clear, encouraging, concrete, honest and local. Speak to the reader as “you.” Explain the money before asking for a decision.

These are the approved Version B quotes. Verify exact wording and attribution with original sources before using them in paid campaigns or printed material. Use humor lightly: “Wanna Play a Game?” fits; shame does not. Describe only features the product actually has.

06 · Rules

Make It Work Everywhere

Consistent controls, spacing, format and accessibility keep the system useful beyond the landing page.

EGP2,000
Primary ActionSecondary Action1,0002,000

Standard button height is 48 px; minimum touch target is 44 px. Use Nile for primary actions, white for secondary actions and a green focus ring on inputs. Selected presets use Nile and white.

  • Maximum content width: 1,120 px. Side padding: 24 px desktop, 16 px narrow mobile.
  • Section rhythm: about 72 px desktop and 48–56 px mobile. Card radius: 22 px; field radius: 16–18 px.
  • Format marketing figures with commas, EGP and no decimals. The calculator starts at 2,000 EGP.
  • Update bank equivalent, monthly amount, annual savings and ten year illustration together. Show an em dash for empty values.
  • Explain rate and compounding assumptions beside the illustration. Actual outcomes are never implied.
  • Keep normal text at WCAG AA contrast or better, preserve visible keyboard focus and do not rely on color alone.
  • Stack calculator panels and secondary metrics on narrow screens. Respect reduced motion settings.
  • Use the bolt in assets/favicon.svg. Prefer simple product diagrams to stock finance photos.
  • Place email signup feedback beside the field. Confirm Google Sheets storage when changing its endpoint; a completed no-cors request alone cannot prove it.

Version B applies these rules in version-b.html and css/version-b.css. Version A remains the comparison page. The survey uses separate styles and should adopt these tokens when it is redesigned.
