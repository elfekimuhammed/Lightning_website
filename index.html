The idea behind Lightning

You Don’t Suck at Budgeting.
You Just Don’t
Have a System.

The you who makes the budget and the you who spends are rarely the same person.

You wake up late, arrive at the office hungry, and buy whatever will help you function. In that moment, your budget won’t even cross your mind.

Stricter budgets, cheaper food and saying no over and over will wear you down. Sooner or later, the budget breaks.

Saving shouldn’t cost you your peace of mind. The key isn’t spending less. It’s understanding why you’re spending in the first place. The problem isn’t the overpriced breakfast. It’s skipping breakfast at home.

Make breakfast at home easier, and watch your spending fall in line with your budget. The budget is the guideline. The habit is what gets you there.

Good questions

Questions, Answered

Is there a phone app?

Not yet. Lightning runs on your computer and opens in your browser. You can add spending as you go, or import your bank statement once a week.

Which computers does it run on?

Windows, Mac and Linux. During early access, setup needs Python 3.11 or newer. The starter script installs everything else.

Which banks can I import from?

Any bank or wallet that lets you download a CSV or spreadsheet. You match the columns once and review every row before it’s saved. Only have a PDF? Lightning gives you ready-made instructions to turn it into a CSV with an AI assistant.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. Lightning never asks for bank logins. You add or import your own activity.

What goes online?

Your balances and transactions stay in one file on your computer, backed up each time Lightning starts. The only thing it looks up online is the latest price of listed shares, by ticker symbol.
