How it works

How Lightning Works,
in Six Simple Steps.

Set it up once. Then check in for a few minutes a week. Every screen below is from the real app, with sample data.

1Add accounts 2Record money 3Set a budget 4Study patterns 5Plan ahead 6See it all
1Once

Add Your Accounts

Add each place your money lives: your bank, your wallet, a CD, THNDR, even the gold at home. Start with today’s balance.

What you do

  1. Name the account and pick its type.
  2. Enter today’s balance.
  3. Repeat for each account.

Good to knowNo need for your full history. Today is a fine place to start.

Add an account

Six accounts, one total.

2As you go

Record Your Money

Type a purchase in seconds: who, what and how much. Or import your bank statement and check it before anything is saved.

What you do

  1. Type the name. Lightning suggests the ones you use.
  2. Pick a category and enter the amount.
  3. Or upload a CSV, review the rows and confirm.
Lightning · CIB Current
Review import

Type it, or import it. Nothing is saved until you confirm.

3Once a month

Set a Budget

Give each category a limit. Then see what you planned, what you spent and what’s left.

What you do

  1. Open a group, like Personal.
  2. Set a fixed amount, a % of income, or your usual average.
  3. Check what’s left as the month goes on.

Good to knowMoney you don’t use can roll over to next month.

Lightning · Budget

Groceries are 170 EGP over. Eating out has 420 EGP left.

4Whenever you wonder

Study Your Patterns

See where your money went and when things changed. This is where habits become visible.

What you do

  1. Pick a period: this month, this year or all time.
  2. Open a category to see what’s inside.
  3. Read the trend to see when it started.

Good to knowFound a habit to change? Give it a limit in step 3.

Lightning · Expense analysis

This year so far: rent and food lead the way.

5Once, then it runs itself

Plan Ahead

Add the bills and loan payments you know are coming, and set money aside for goals. Lightning tells you what’s safe to spend.

What you do

  1. Add bills and subscriptions with their due dates.
  2. Add loans and how many payments are left.
  3. Set aside money for goals, like an emergency fund.

Good to knowWhen a bill is paid, Lightning can match it to the transaction.

6Anytime

See the Whole Picture

Cash, deposits, THNDR shares, funds and gold, all in EGP. See what you own, and what it would be worth if you sold today.

What you do

  1. Record your buys, sells and dividends.
  2. Update prices when you like.
  3. Open Birdview to see how it all adds up.

Good to knowHolding money for family? Mark it, and it stays out of your net worth.
