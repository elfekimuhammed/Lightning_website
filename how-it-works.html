How it works

From Scattered Money
to One Clear Picture.

Seven steps, one afternoon to set up, a few minutes a week to keep going. Every screen below comes straight from the Lightning app, using sample data for one household in Cairo.

1Add your accounts 2Log what moves 3Import a statement 4Set your plan 5Study your patterns 6Plan your cash 7See your wealth
1Set up · about 10 minutes

Add Every Place Your Money Lives

Start with where your money is today. A current account at CIB, savings at NBE, a CD at Banque Misr, your THNDR portfolio, the wallet in your pocket and the gold at home each become one account with today’s balance.

What you do

  1. Name the account and choose its type: bank, cash, deposit, brokerage or physical asset.
  2. Enter today’s balance and the date you start tracking.
  3. Repeat for each account. Lightning totals them in the sidebar, in EGP.

TipYou don’t need your full history. Starting from today’s balance is enough to see this month clearly.

2Every day · seconds per entry

Log What Moves, the Way You Say It

Open an account and type straight into its register: who you paid, what it was for and how much. Lightning remembers your counterparties, so “Tal…” already knows you mean Talabat.

What you do

  1. Type the counterparty, then pick the suggestion or create a new one.
  2. Choose a category, such as Eating Out or Transportation.
  3. Enter the amount. Negative for money out, positive for money in.

TipMoving money between your own accounts, like an ATM withdrawal, is a transfer. It never counts as spending.

Lightning · CIB Current

3Weekly or monthly · a few minutes

Import a Statement, Review Before It Counts

When typing is too slow, bring in your bank’s CSV. Match the columns once, then review every row. Lightning flags names that look like ones you already use and rows that look like duplicates.

What you do

  1. Choose Import CSV on the account and upload the file.
  2. Match Date, Counterparty and Amount. Category and Notes are optional.
  3. Settle anything that needs a decision, then post the ready rows.

TipNothing is saved until you confirm. Tick “Remember category” and the next statement sorts itself.

4Once a month · 5 minutes

Set a Plan That Fits Your Month

Give each category a limit. Use a fixed amount, a share of your income, or let Lightning use your own 3 or 6 month average. The budget shows what you planned, what you spent and what is left.

What you do

  1. Open a spending group, such as Personal.
  2. Set each category as fixed EGP, % of income, or an average.
  3. Check “Left in plan” during the month. Green means room; rose means over.

TipUnused plan can carry over to next month, so a quiet week is never wasted.

5Whenever you wonder · 2 minutes

Study Your Patterns

This is where habits become visible. Expense analysis answers two questions: where did it go, and when did it change? Drill from a group to a category to the transactions behind it.

What you do

  1. Choose a period: this month, year to date, all time or custom.
  2. Select a row to drill down, all the way to single transactions.
  3. Read the monthly trend to spot when something started.

TipFound a habit you want to change? Go back to step 4 and give it a limit.

Lightning · Expense analysis

Year to date, Housing and Food lead; the trend shows each month side by side.

6Once, then automatic

Plan Your Cash Before It Is Spent

Add the bills, subscriptions and loan payments you know are coming. Set money aside for an emergency fund or a trip. Lightning takes all of it off first and shows what is safe to spend.

What you do

  1. Add recurring bills and subscriptions with their due dates.
  2. Add loans with the payment and the number of payments left.
  3. Create reserves for goals and set aside what you can.

TipMark a bill as paid, or let Lightning match it to the transaction when it arrives.

7Anytime · one glance

See Your Whole Wealth in One Place

Birdview puts it together: cash, deposits, THNDR shares and funds, and gold valued by the gram. Update prices when you like, and see what everything would be worth if you sold today.

What you do

  1. Record buys, sells and dividends on your brokerage account.
  2. Update prices for shares, funds and gold.
  3. Open Birdview to see how what you own adds up.

TipHolding money for your mother or a sibling? Mark it as held for others, and it stays out of your net worth.

Then it becomes a habit

A Loop, Not a Chore

After setup, Lightning runs on a simple rhythm. Log as you go, review once a week, adjust once a month. Each turn of the loop makes the next month a little clearer.
