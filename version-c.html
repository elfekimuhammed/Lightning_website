A better system

You Don’t Suck at Budgeting. You Don’t Have a System.

You might convince yourself that you do have a system by creating a budget, but the two are not the same.

You on a Friday, budgeting your way to financial freedom, and you on a stressful Tuesday, ordering a vanilla shake to feel better, are not the same person.

You don’t save more by writing better budgets. You save more by understanding your spending patterns and cutting out what you can live without.

Study your patterns, and create better habits.

Or else your stressed out version will keep cursing you and your budget.
