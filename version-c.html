A better system

You Don’t Suck at Budgeting. You Just Don’t Have a System.

The you who makes the budget and the you who spends are rarely the same person.

You wake up late, arrive at the office hungry, and buy whatever will help you function. In that moment, your budget won’t even cross your mind.

Stricter budgets, cheaper food and saying no over and over will wear you down. Sooner or later, the budget breaks.

Saving shouldn’t cost you your peace of mind. The key isn’t spending less. It’s understanding why you’re spending in the first place. The problem isn’t the overpriced breakfast. It’s skipping breakfast at home.

Make breakfast at home easier, and watch your spending fall in line with your budget. The budget is the guideline. The habit is what gets you there.
