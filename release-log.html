Day by Day

Beta 1 is the download. The two newer days arrive with the next build.

2026-10-03

Next build · in testing

Big

  • Faster. Budget (all time) opens in 0.41 s, was 7.0 s. The Overview opens in 0.24 s, was 0.96 s.
  • Money with others. A Held for others page, Change ownership, and “Someone paid an expense for you”. None of it counts in what you own.

New

  • Export for AI. Settings › Your data makes one workbook of your numbers, plus a prompt. Nothing is uploaded. Being merged.
  • Get set up card. Five first-run steps that tick themselves.
  • Bulk set a category on selected register rows.

Visual

  • Brand fonts and the two-leaf logo inside the app.
  • Cards coloured by meaning, and a Back route on every detail page.

Fixed

  • Import review. One answer per name (17, not 43 rows).
  • Same figure everywhere. Overview and Budget agree on what is left in your plan.
  • Stale prices flagged when older than about two months.

2026-10-02

Next build · in testing

Big

  • Check against bank. Type the bank’s balance. A small gap settles with one adjustment row.
  • Certificates. A portfolio per bank, terms per CD, interest shown as an estimate.

New

  • Start fresh. A new profile, with the old one untouched.
  • Export selected rows to CSV.

Visual

  • Our own calendar on every date field.
  • Day by day and Month by month heatmaps.

Fixed

  • Dropdowns open inside popups again.

2026-10-01

0.4.0 beta 1 · the download

Big

  • Windows app with encrypted profiles, a password and a recovery key.

New

  • CSV import up to 5 MiB, reviewed before anything posts.
  • Verified backup before any upgrade.
  • Version shown in the title bar and Settings.

Visual

  • Key notes that lead with the number.
  • Right-click row actions in registers.

Still open

Known gaps

  • An “Other” asset (like a flat) can count twice in holdings.
  • Recording something you already owned counts as a net worth change.
  • Safe to spend ignores bills due right after payday.
  • Selling everything in a period hides its gain on the Investments tab.
  • No legacy import or backup restore in the app yet. Use dummy data.
