Money With Others

Cash and investments you hold for someone else stay out of what you own.

  • Held for others page. Cash and investments per person and account, with a history of every change.
  • Change ownership. Move cash between your share and a saved person. The account’s total balance does not change.
  • Someone paid an expense for you. It counts in your spending and budget, and the matching cash share is credited to whoever paid.
  • Checked on the dates affected. Edits, voids and restores check each person’s balance, so nobody goes negative by accident.
2026-10-03 · built, in testing

Export For AI

Settings › Your data prepares one workbook of your own numbers for an AI of your choice.

  • One .xlsx file. Choose All time, YTD, a month or a custom range. It holds Lightning’s own totals, your owned transaction lines, investment checkpoints and the category list.
  • A prompt that goes with it. It is copied for you and you can edit it before you paste.
  • Nothing is uploaded. The file stays on your computer. You decide where it goes.
  • Not capped. Every line in the period is there, and notes cannot run as spreadsheet formulas.
2026-10-03 · built, being merged into the main build

Import A Statement With Fewer Decisions

Rows with the same name are one block, so you answer once per name.

  • Once per name. In the sample year that is 17 answers instead of 43 rows.
  • Pick a category by group. Every choice sits under its group, and typing filters by group and name.
  • Discard or finish later. A statement still in review can be discarded, or left and continued from Import CSV.
  • Bulk set a category. Select rows in a register and set one category on all of them. Transfers and held-for-others rows are left alone, and the message says how many changed.
2026-10-03

Check Against Your Bank

Type the balance the bank shows on a date. That is the whole check.

  • Small differences settle themselves. Up to 1% of the bank balance or 100 EGP, whichever is larger, becomes one balance adjustment row.
  • Bigger differences are sent to the right place. The check links to that month’s register or to Import CSV.
  • Replaces line-by-line clearing. The sample year’s October check was 62 clicks. It is one typed balance now.
2026-10-02

Certificates And Time Deposits

Each bank has its own CD portfolio, and each certificate its own terms.

  • Terms. Principal, start, earliest withdrawal, maturity, rate, simple or compound, and where the money goes.
  • Maturity as a date or a term. Enter years in three-month steps and the date is worked out for you.
  • Purchases are checked. The cash must be there on the purchase date, and later balances cannot go negative.
  • Estimates, never posted. Interest and maturity cash appear in Cash planning as estimates. Lightning does not post interest. You record the real amount from your statement.
  • If you sold today. A CD counts only after its earliest withdrawal date, at the early-redemption estimate until maturity.

Honest Numbers

Where two screens used to disagree, there is now one figure.

  • Left in plan. The Overview and Budget show the same figure. In the sample September both now read 11,853 EGP left of 38,898. The Overview used to say −2,317 of 24,698.
  • Estimates say so. A small “!” explains when a budget estimate comes from thin data, for example one month of spending.
  • Out-of-date prices. Holdings valued from a price more than about two months old are flagged on the Overview and on the holding.
  • Needs you. The Overview’s list now says what is over plan and which goals are past their date.

Dates, Calendars And Heatmaps

One calendar everywhere, and your month at a glance.

  • Lightning’s own calendar. Weeks run Monday to Sunday. It works from the keyboard and inside popups.
  • Day by day. Expense analysis ends with the period as a calendar: spending on the left, net cash flow on the right.
  • Month by month. The Overview ends with the same view by month, one row per year. Each month opens its Expense analysis.
  • The period you pick stays picked. It carries across Overview, Budget, Expense analysis and Investments.

A First Run That Guides You

The Overview shows a Get set up card until the basics are in.

  • Five steps. Accounts, history, salary and bills, an emergency fund, and a budget. Each one ticks itself from your data and the card disappears when all five are done.
  • Honest when empty. Without a salary and bills, Cash planning asks for them instead of showing a forecast built from nothing.
  • Start fresh. Settings › Your data can open a new profile and leave the old one exactly as it is.
  • Export selected. Check rows in a register, Categories or Reevaluations and export just those to CSV, up to 1,000.

Lightning 0.4.0 Beta 1

The build you can download today.

Your Data Stays Yours

The first Windows app, with a profile for each person or purpose.

  • Lightning.exe for Windows. No Python and no install steps beyond unzipping.
  • Encrypted profiles. Each profile lives in Documents/Lightning, opens with a password, and has a recovery key shown once at setup.
  • Safe upgrades. A verified backup is made before any upgrade changes your data.
  • Version in the title bar. And in Settings, so feedback names the right build.
2026-10-01

Read Your Money

Five pages that each answer one question.

  • Overview. Net worth, free cash, what it is made of, and what needs you.
  • Budget. Planned, spent, then left in plan, by category.
  • Cash planning. Safe to spend until payday, bills and income that repeat, loans, and reserves with your emergency fund.
  • Investments. Buy, sell and dividends, prices you type or fetch, and the period’s gain or loss.
  • Expense analysis. Where it went, compared with your usual month.
2026-09-30 to 2026-10-01

Bring Your Own Statement

Import a bank CSV and review it before anything is posted.

  • CSV only. One signed amount column, or separate money in and out columns, up to 5 MiB. PDFs are not supported.
  • Nothing posts until you review it. Duplicates are flagged and similar names are suggestions you pick, never merged silently.
  • Private while it is read. The file is held in memory and not written to a temporary file.
2026-10-01

A fictional household, so you never need your own data.

  • A sample household. Omar, a salaried user in Cairo, with a year of fictional statements, prices and a certificate.
  • Open it from the welcome page. Or download the sample CSVs from Current Status and import them yourself.
  • Use dummy data in this beta. Do not upload a real bank statement.

Known Gaps

What is open, so you know where to look.

Known Gaps We Are Fixing

These are open, and we would rather say so.

  • Other assets can be counted twice. An “Other” asset such as a flat can appear in both brokerage cash and holdings, so two screens may differ.
  • Recording something you already owned. It currently counts as a change in net worth.
  • Safe to spend the day before payday. It does not yet subtract bills due right after payday.
  • Sold everything in a period. The Investments tab shows “No holdings yet” and hides that period’s gain. The Overview still shows it.

Not In The App Yet

What the beta cannot do today.

  • Bringing in your old data. Legacy import is not in the screens yet.
  • Restoring a backup. Backups are written, but restore is not in the screens yet.
  • Ordinary-PC acceptance. The app is still being checked on everyday Windows machines.
  • Next on our list. Simpler registers with two-line rows, clearer Reserves and emergency-fund wording, and a words-and-numbers sweep.
